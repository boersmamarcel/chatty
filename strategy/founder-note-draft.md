## Long version (About page, ~250 words)

**Why I built Chatty**

AI assistants got good enough to do real work: not just answer questions, but open the files, run the analysis, write the document. That's exciting. It also means handing a lot of trust to software you can't see into, running on models you didn't choose, in someone else's cloud, on a monthly subscription.

I wanted the opposite. An AI coworker that:

- **shows its work**, so I can watch every step, see every change and see what it cost;
- **lets me stay in charge**: it asks before it acts, and I can stop it or take over at any moment;
- **checks before it says "done"**: it looks at what it built and re-runs the tests;
- **stays mine**: my files on my computer, any model I choose (including ones that run entirely on my laptop), and no account.

That's Chatty. I use it every day [describe your real use: e.g. "to debug builds, write reports from data, and run a small team of agents on my own projects"].
